## As built (Phase 5)
Layout: gold rule · category + entry index ("MOON · 03 / 07", same numbering as the journal) · name + stamp · data row (mission · year · status)
· two columns: **ARCHIVE RECORD** (purpose, size, account, sources; gold badge) | **FIELD READINGS · IN-GAME** (stratum, depth, signal, measured, estimated,
setting; red SIM badge). No fact appears twice. Missing data is hidden, never replaced by an invented default.
Actions: `CONTINUE` (Esc) ghost · `WATCH STORY` (E) primary, initial focus. Keyboard is ignored for 0.8 s after opening so a held E cannot skip it.
Motion: card fade (200 ms) then stamp press (scale 1.6→1, −12°→−4°); reduced motion = instant.
