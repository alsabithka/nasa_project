# Echoes Beyond Earth — AI Development Rules
Project: Godot 4.x educational 3D exploration game.
Language: GDScript only.

Architecture rules:
- Follow the existing folder and scene structure.
- Reuse existing shared systems.
- Follow documentation/INTERFACES.md.
- Keep artifact content data-driven.
- Do not create duplicate managers or registries.

Collaboration rules:
- Modify only files owned by your assigned workstream.
- Do not change project.godot or Autoloads without approval.
- Do not rename shared functions or signals without coordination.
- Do not overwrite another developer's work.
- Report every created or modified file.

Before declaring a task complete, report:
1. Files changed.
2. What was implemented.
3. How it connects to other systems.
4. How to test it in Godot.
5. Known limitations and assumptions.
